# LazyChef & Pantry Autopilot Ubiquitous Language

Domain vocabulary for the LazyChef & Pantry Autopilot kitchen assistant project, establishing consistent terminology across documentation and code.

## Language

**Sloth Constraints**:
Strict operational limits enforcing maximum one cooking vessel, maximum three steps, and under fifteen minutes preparation time to eliminate culinary effort.
_Avoid_: lazy rules, easy filters, quick shortcuts

**The Lazy Payoff**:
The quantified savings in minutes of decision fatigue and grocery waste cost achieved by following minimal-effort meal recommendations.
_Avoid_: ROI, time saved score, efficiency metric

**Pantry State**:
The client-side local inventory of currently available food items, staples, and condiments stored without cloud synchronization.
_Avoid_: inventory list, food stock, grocery stash

**Lazy Recipe**:
A single deterministic or AI-generated meal recommendation adhering strictly to Sloth Constraints and utilizing existing pantry items.
_Avoid_: quick meal, simple dish, fast food

**Grocery Gap**:
The minimal subset of missing essential staples or complementary items flagged for optional replenishment.
_Avoid_: missing shopping items, staple shortfall, buy list
